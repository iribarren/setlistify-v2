Reserved regions. Each dashed panel is a labeled placeholder, not a mockup of the future feature — it exists so prompt 19–21 slot in without moving anything else on the page. Past vs. upcoming, no playlist. This artboard shows the past case: the show happened but setlist.fm has nothing yet, so the copy is active ("we'll keep checking" + a manual recheck action) — there is something to wait for. An upcoming concert's reserved playlist region reads differently: "Playlist unlocks once the show happens" with no recheck action at all, because there is nothing to check — the setlist can't exist before the gig. That distinction is the answer to the open question in the brief: same slot, different copy and affordance depending on why it's empty.
