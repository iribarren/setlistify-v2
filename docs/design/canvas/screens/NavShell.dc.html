Why only two destinations today. "Concerts" and "Account" are the only things this product does right now — Playlists, Notes and Sharing (prompts 15–21) each earn a nav entry only once they ship; the tab bar and sidebar both have room to grow to 3–4 items without redesign, but nothing is stubbed in now to avoid promising a feature that isn't there. Breakpoints. Persistent sidebar ≥1120px (used in every desktop artboard in this canvas); a collapsed icon-only rail from 900–1119px keeps navigation reachable without eating content width; below 900px (tablet portrait and phone-in-landscape edge cases) it becomes a drawer behind a menu icon; true phone width uses the bottom tab bar exclusively, never the drawer, since thumb reach matters more than screen space there.
