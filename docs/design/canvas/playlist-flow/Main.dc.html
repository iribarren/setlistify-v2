Recommendation — no forced choice. Fast is the one-tap default: tapping "Generate playlist" directly starts Fast mode, no picker in the way. The choice only surfaces for someone who wants control, via a small link underneath — never a wall of two equal buttons that makes everyone stop and decide. On desktop there's room to expand that link inline into two cards without leaving the page; on phone it opens as a bottom sheet (Components.dc.html's sheet pattern), because a full navigation away for a decision this small would be heavier than the decision itself. Copy stays jargon-free: "Fast" / "Choose it yourself", not "Fast mode" / "Normal mode" — those labels are ours, not the user's.
