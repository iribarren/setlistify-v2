09 · Iconography

Direction & source

Lucide (MIT license, ships as SVGs and as lucide-react-native) — a single consistent stroke-based line set, no filled/outline mismatch across the app. 1.5px stroke at 24px, 1.75px at 20px and below. Round caps and joins throughout; no icon is ever recolored per-instance beyond the semantic text tokens on the Palette artboard.

Chosen over a custom-drawn set: Lucide already covers everything below, embeds natively on all three platforms without a web-font icon hack, and its restrained geometry sits comfortably next to Petrona/Manrope without competing for attention the way a heavier duotone set would.

No emoji anywhere in the product UI — Setlistify's warmth comes from typography, color and the ticket-stub motif, not glyphs. Status glyphs (matched / needs review / error) reuse the same three shapes as the state artboards, so a user learns them once.
