Setlistify — Design System

02 · Typography

Display / headings — Petrona

Camden, 14 Sep

Petrona, ui-serif, Georgia, 'Times New Roman', serif

A warm literary serif for concert names, section headers and the hero moment on a concert page — reads like a printed program, not a dashboard title.

UI / body — Manrope

19 songs matched

Manrope, ui-sans-serif, system-ui, -apple-system, 'Segoe UI', Roboto, sans-serif

Everything functional: buttons, labels, list rows, form fields, body copy. Geometric-humanist, high legibility at small sizes on all three platforms.

Ticket data — Space Mono

GA · £38.50

'Space Mono', ui-monospace, 'SFMono-Regular', Menlo, monospace

Dates, prices, timestamps, match-count badges — the stamped, tabular feel of a real ticket stub. Used sparingly, never for body copy.
