On mobile, the same modal content renders as a bottom sheet (radius-lg on the top two corners only, full width, drag handle) rather than a centered card — same tokens, different container. Focus states use a 3px accent-primary ring at 15% opacity, applied consistently across inputs, buttons and tabs, never color alone (see Accessibility).
